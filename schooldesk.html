Systems

SchoolDesk

Admin built for schools

Fees, enrolment, learner records, messaging, and timetabling on one disciplined stack—fewer frantic WhatsApp broadcasts and quieter finance offices.

SchoolDesk is Nutekh’s administration spine for institutions where stewardship matters: guardians expect clarity on fees and safety; teachers need timetables and registers that do not compete with five offline notebooks; finance teams must prove every shilling.

Institution fit

Who SchoolDesk serves

Private K–12 & academies

Structured terms, repeatable fee plans, guardian relationships, sibling links, and class lists that stay current when enrolment churns mid-year.

Faith-based & community schools

Bursary workflows, sponsor tracking, and communication trees that respect governance boards and volunteer administrators.

Small chains & groups

Shared policies with campus-level tweaks, central reporting for owners, and consolidated fee intelligence without manual consolidation sheets.

Product depth

Operational modules

Configured to statutory and donor requirements agreed in discovery—Nutekh does not bypass your board’s policies.

Admissions & records

  • Pipeline stages from inquiry to placement—owners see conversion without ad-hoc spreadsheets.
  • Learner profile with guardians, medical notes (where policy allows), and document attachments.
  • Class placement & promotions with history so academic leads can explain decisions.

Fees & finance office

  • Fee structures: annual, termly, instalments, discounts, sibling rules, and special cases with approval trails.
  • Invoicing & receipts with clear audit trail—who posted, who edited, and why.
  • Arrears workflows: ageing, promises to pay, and gentle escalation paths (policy stays yours).
  • Payment rails: cash, bank, card, mobile money—mapped to reconciliation expectations.

Attendance & safeguarding hooks

  • Daily registers with late slips and early dismissals.
  • Notification triggers for unexplained absences—subject to parent opt-in and local law.

Timetables & assessments

  • Period structures, room assignments, and conflict checks for teachers wearing multiple hats.
  • Exam windows & results capture with export paths for boards when required.

Parent & staff communication

  • WhatsApp-aware workflows where policy permits—templated updates, delivery receipts, and audit of broadcast vs personal threads.
  • Portal or lightweight web views for statements and notices when guardians prefer self-serve.
  • Role segregation so teachers cannot see fee negotiation notes unless authorised.
Governance

Transparency for boards & bursars

SchoolDesk reporting is designed around questions boards actually ask:

  • Cash vs recognition—what was collected versus what remains outstanding?
  • Leakage alerts—unusual write-offs or manual overrides by user.
  • Per-class economics when fee subsidies differ across streams.

Where schools also run enterprises (cafeterias, transport), Nutekh can align commercial income reporting with broader finance systems such as ImaliCloud—scope is explicitly agreed.

FAQ

Common questions

Can parents pay only by mobile money?

SchoolDesk can reflect the payment mix your office accepts. Nutekh maps each channel to reconciliation tasks your bursar can defend in audit.

What about student data privacy?

Access controls, retention policy, and guardian consent flows are configured to your board’s direction—Nutekh documents the model; legal sign-off remains yours.

Do you replace our Learning Management System?

SchoolDesk focuses on administration and finance touchpoints. LMS integration may be scoped separately when both exist.
