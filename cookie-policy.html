This Cookie Policy explains how Nutekh ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. It explains what cookies are, why we use them, and how you can control them.

This policy complies with the EU ePrivacy Directive, the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and the GDPR.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They help the website recognize your device and remember information about your visit. Cookies can be "persistent" (remain after you close your browser) or "session" (deleted when you close your browser).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Duration
Essential Required for basic website functionality, such as security and navigation. Session / Persistent
Analytics Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., pages visited, time on site). We use anonymized data only. Persistent (up to 2 years)
Preferences Remember your settings and preferences (e.g., language, region). Persistent (up to 1 year)

4. How to Control Cookies

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can choose to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You may also change your preferences at any time.

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is set.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the proper functioning of the website.

To learn how to manage cookies in your browser, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (such as Google Analytics) that set their own cookies. These third-party services have their own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control these cookies.

By continuing to use our website after seeing the cookie banner, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, subject to the preferences you select. You may withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings or cookie preferences.
