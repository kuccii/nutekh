The Nutekh Mission

A tech team that understands East African business.

Many businesses in Tanzania and East Africa still run on paper, WhatsApp, and manual Excel sheets. Nutekh helps you move step-by-step into the digital world — without confusing technical language.

We design simple systems that match how people really work here: unstable internet, mobile-first staff, cash and mobile money payments, and customers who prefer WhatsApp and phone calls.

Whether you run a shop in Kariakoo, a clinic in Arusha, a transport company, a school, or a growing NGO, we help you use technology to save time, reduce mistakes, and serve your customers better.
