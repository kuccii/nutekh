# Nutekh — Company Brief

This brief is injected into every assistant prompt. It is the source of truth for
core facts. Anything not covered here must be answered from retrieved knowledge or
escalated to a human.

## Who we are

Nutekh is a digital transformation agency headquartered in Kigali, Rwanda,
serving businesses across East Africa. We help teams deploy, integrate, and scale
practical systems for online presence, operations, marketing, and payments.

We work with retailers, hospitality businesses, healthcare providers, logistics
companies, schools, and NGOs.

## What we do (services)

- **Websites & presence** — a fast website plus verified Google and social profiles.
- **WhatsApp automation (Rainmaker)** — greetings, FAQs, lead capture, qualification,
  polite follow-ups, and warm-prospect hand-off to staff.
- **Business-process automation** — invoicing, stock checks, approvals, reminders, and
  payout alerts.
- **Payments & reconciliation** — MTN/Airtel mobile money, POS/card readers, and bank
  feeds matched in the background.
- **ImaliCloud** — cash, payroll, and inventory in one cloud.
- **Hresa** — QR ordering, kitchen tickets, and fast check-out for venues.
- **VUKA** — Hresa guest flow plus ImaliCloud books for hotels and property.
- **CRM & lead pipeline** — one pipeline, with one paid channel (Meta or Google).
- **Dashboards & reporting** — owner/funder dashboards, spending controls, and
  investor/lender reporting (Scale).

## How we help

Nutekh works in three plain-language tracks:

1. **Deploy** — get the business visible, credible, and running on a real system.
2. **Integrate** — connect sales, stock, mobile money, bank feeds, and WhatsApp so
   money and inventory tell one story.
3. **Scale** — add dashboards, controls, and reporting that owners and funders can
   trust.

Four focus areas:

- **Presence** — a clear website plus verified Google and social profiles, so the
  business is trusted on first look.
- **Automate** — invoicing, stock checks, approvals, reminders, and payout alerts run
  in software instead of manual loops.
- **Market** — sharper positioning, simple creative, WhatsApp greetings, FAQs, and
  polite follow-ups that pass warm prospects to humans.
- **Connect** — POS, card readers, MTN/Airtel mobile money, and bank deposits stay
  matched in the background, with fewer double entries.

## Products

- **Hresa** — guest flow for busy venues: QR ordering, readable kitchen tickets, and
  fast check-out.
- **ImaliCloud** — finance and stock in one cloud: cash, payroll, and inventory.
- **Rainmaker** — WhatsApp-led growth: capture leads, filter tyre-kickers, follow up
  politely, and send serious buyers to staff. Paid ads are added only after the free
  path shows collected money.
- **VUKA** — operations for upscale stays, hotels, and property.

## How engagements run

1. Free 30-minute audit or planning call.
2. Discovery workshop: map current tools, payment rails, and pain points.
3. Supervised configuration, rehearsals, and parallel runs where needed.
4. Training that matches how teams actually work.
5. Go-live, then optional ongoing support under a retainer.

Deliveries are phased. We design for mobile-first teams, WhatsApp-heavy
communication, intermittent internet, and mixed payment rails.
