Systems

Heba / Hresa

Built for slammed service—not generic retail tills

Guests, kitchen, payouts, and stock stay linked—from QR menus to night-close—even when Wi‑Fi blinks.

Heba is Nutekh’s hospitality layer around Hresa: orders, kitchen pacing, recipe-driven stock usage, payouts stay connected so nightly margin is readable—not trapped in spreadsheets until month-end.

Where it earns its keep

Styles of service we support

Quick service & coffee

High ticket volume, modifiers, and hold/fire patterns. Heba emphasises tap speed, fewer mis-taps, and clear void/comp reasons for audit.

Full-service dining

Table plans, coursing, split bills, service charges, and gratuity distribution rules—without wait staff juggling three apps.

Multi-branch & cloud kitchens

Central menu pushes with local overrides, brand-level reporting, and delivery-channel tagging so margin by channel is visible.

Product depth

Modules & floor discipline

Configured to match how you operate—Nutekh typically installs the bundles below rather than flipping every toggle on week one.

Front of house — POS & service

  • Order modes: dine-in, takeaway, delivery, tabs, and quick-sale where needed.
  • Modifiers & holds: nested options, priced add-ons, allergy flags, and kitchen notes that actually print in the right language.
  • Table management: merge/split, transfer, and course firing aligned to your SOP.
  • Discount policies: role-gated comps, manager PINs, and reason codes for audit.

Kitchen — KDS & production

  • Kitchen display routing by station (grill, cold, bar) with bump/recall and overdue visibility.
  • Prep lists & par levels where your operation uses them—especially for high-SKU venues.
  • Recipe cards linked to stock depletion so theoretical usage tracks reality.

Menu & pricing

  • Time-based pricing (happy hour), channel-specific menus, and limited-time offers with start/stop controls.
  • Multi-language labels for kitchen chits when teams are mixed.
  • Product bundling and meal deals with clear margin guardrails.

Stock & purchasing

  • Recipe-driven depletion with wastage buckets and batch prep tracking.
  • Supplier catalogues, receiving, and variance notes on delivery.
  • Stock takes with blind counts and approval steps for high-variance lines.

People & shifts

  • Shift templates, clock-in rules, and role permissions (cashier vs supervisor vs kitchen).
  • Tip pooling options where policy allows—implementation follows your HR guidance.

Payments & close

  • Tenders: cash, card terminals, mobile money, voucher, and split payments.
  • Reconciliation helpers: expected vs deposited, shift reports, and exception queues.
  • Night-close checklist with sign-off roles so leadership trusts the Z-report.
Delivery & demand

WhatsApp confirmations

FAQ

Common questions

Does Heba work when the internet drops?

Nutekh designs offline/online rules per site—what can still be sold, what must pause, and how sync conflicts resolve. Details are captured in your runbook, not left to chance.

Can we keep our existing payment provider?

Often yes, subject to device/API availability. We document reconciliation between provider reports and Heba shifts.

How long is a typical implementation?

Simple QSR: a few weeks. Multi-branch with delivery integrations: longer. Your statement of work lists milestones—not generic promises.
